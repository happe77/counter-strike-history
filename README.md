# Counter-Strike History

A curated collection of projects, communities, tools, and individuals that have helped shape Counter-Strike throughout its different generations.

## Areas
- Community infrastructure
- SourceMod / AMXX
- Mapping & workshop ecosystems
- Game modding
- Competitive Counter-Strike
- Server communities
- Tools & utilities
- Counter-Strike history & legacy
- Organizations
- Analysts
- Coaches

## Purpose
Highlighting and documenting parts of the Counter-Strike ecosystem and the people behind them — from early community-driven eras to the modern scene.
